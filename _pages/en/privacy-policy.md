This policy explains which personal data may be processed when you visit xbaro.eu (the “Website”), for what purpose, for how long and what rights you have. Cookies are described in the [cookie policy]({{ base_path }}/en/cookie-policy/).

## 1. Controller

Xavier Baró, in a personal capacity. Contact: [xbaro@ub.edu](mailto:xbaro@ub.edu). This is a personal website and does not represent any institution.

## 2. What data is processed, why and on what legal basis

| Processing | Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Web analytics** (Google Analytics 4), **only if you press “Approve”** on the banner | Pseudonymous browser identifier (cookies `_ga`, `_ga_*`), pages visited, referrer, browser type, device and operating system, language, approximate location derived from the IP address (which Google does not store in full) and date and time | Knowing which pages are viewed and improving the Website | Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR and Art. 22.2 LSSI). You can withdraw it at any time |
| **Usage events** (also with consent) | Which publication link is followed (PDF, HTML, DOI, URL) and when a BibTeX reference is viewed, copied or downloaded, with the work's title, key and DOI | Knowing which publications are of interest | Consent |
| **Server logs** | IP address, date and time and requested page, generated by the host (GitHub Pages) | Security and technical operation of the service | Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) |
| **Email** | What you send me when you write to me | Replying to you | Legitimate interest or your request |

If you **deny** the banner, Google Analytics and any other third-party script are not loaded and no identifier is associated with you. No processing involves automated decisions or advertising profiling; advertising purposes are denied.

No special categories of data are collected and visitors are not asked for data: the Website has no forms, accounts or comments.

## 3. Recipients and international transfers

- **Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC** (Google Analytics), as processor, only if you have accepted. It may process data in the United States; the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. More information in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub, Inc.** (GitHub Pages), which hosts the Website and sees its technical access logs. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

I do not share your data with any other third party.

## 4. Retention

Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for the retention period configured on the property (at most 14 months) and is then deleted automatically. `_ga` cookies expire after 2 years at most and `cookie-notice-option` after 31 days. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

## 5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), by writing to [xbaro@ub.edu](mailto:xbaro@ub.edu). To withdraw it from the Website, use the “Change my choice” button in the cookie policy.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es).

## 6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always this page, with the date of the last modification.
