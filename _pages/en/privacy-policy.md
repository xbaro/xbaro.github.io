This policy explains which personal data may be processed when you visit xbaro.eu (the “Website”), for what purpose, for how long and what rights you have. Cookies are described in the [cookie policy]({{ base_path }}/en/cookie-policy/).

## 1. Controller

Xavier Baró, in a personal capacity. Contact: [xbaro@ub.edu](mailto:xbaro@ub.edu). This is a personal website and does not represent any institution.

## 2. What data is processed, why and on what legal basis

| Processing | Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Web analytics** (Google Analytics 4), **only if you press “Approve”** on the banner | Pseudonymous browser identifier (cookies `_ga`, `_ga_*`), pages visited, referrer, browser type, device and operating system, language, approximate location derived from the IP address (Google uses it only for this and does not log or store it) and date and time | Knowing which pages are viewed and improving the Website | Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR and Art. 22.2 LSSI). You can withdraw it at any time |
| **Usage events** (also with consent) | Which publication link is followed (PDF, HTML, DOI, URL) and when a BibTeX reference is viewed, copied or downloaded, with the work's title, key and DOI | Knowing which publications are of interest | Consent |
| **Hosting access logs** | Visitors' IP addresses, which GitHub logs for security purposes | Security of the service. This is processing by GitHub, under its own responsibility | See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) |
| **Email** | What you send me when you write to me | Replying to you | Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): replying to correspondence |

If you **deny** the banner, Google Analytics and any other third-party script are not loaded and no identifier is associated with you. No processing involves automated decisions or advertising profiling; advertising purposes are denied.

No special categories of data are collected and visitors are not asked for data: the Website has no forms, accounts or comments.

## 3. Recipients and international transfers

- **Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC** (Google Analytics), as processor, only if you have accepted. It may process data in the United States; the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. The EU–US framework relies on a Commission adequacy decision that may be reviewed or annulled; in that case the standard clauses would apply. More information in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub, Inc.** (GitHub Pages), which hosts the Website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under its own responsibility. According to its privacy statement, GitHub relies on the Data Privacy Framework and, in general, on standard contractual clauses for transfers to the United States. The retention period of these logs is determined by GitHub. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

I do not share your data with any other third party.

## 4. Retention

User- and event-level analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for at most 14 months (property setting, not renewed when the visitor returns) and then deleted; aggregated statistical reports, without identifiers, are kept. `_ga` cookies have a default lifetime of 2 years and `cookie-notice-option` of 31 days. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

## 5. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), by writing to [xbaro@ub.edu](mailto:xbaro@ub.edu). To withdraw it from the Website, use the “Change my choice” button in the cookie policy.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es).

## 6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always this page, with the date of the last modification.
