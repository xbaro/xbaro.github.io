This policy explains which cookies and similar technologies xbaro.eu (the “Website”) uses, what they are for and how you can control them.

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They make a website work, remember your preferences or measure how it is used.

## 2. Cookies we use

**Necessary first-party cookie.** `cookie-notice-option` stores your banner choice (accept or reject) for 31 days. It contains no personal data and cannot be switched off, because without it the banner would reappear on every visit.

**Analytics cookies (only if you accept).** If you press “Approve”, Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID `G-ENN14SG31P`) is loaded, which allows the use of the Website to be measured. This service may store cookies such as `_ga` and `_ga_*` (up to 2 years) to tell visitors and sessions apart and produce aggregated statistics. It also counts, without identifying you, which publication links are followed and how many times a BibTeX reference is viewed, copied or downloaded. If you press “Deny”, nothing from Google is loaded.

**Advertising.** We do not use advertising or ad-personalisation cookies; those purposes are denied in the consent settings.

## 3. Consent

The first time you visit the Website you will see a cookie banner. No analytics tool or third-party script is loaded until you press “Approve”. You can change your mind at any time:

<p><button type="button" class="btn btn--inverse" onclick="document.cookie='cookie-notice-option=; expires=Thu, 01 Jan 1970 00:00:00 GMT; path=/'; location.reload();">Change my choice</button> <small>Clears the saved choice and shows the cookie banner again.</small></p>

You can also manage or delete cookies in your browser settings (view, delete, block them, or get notifications).

## 4. Third parties

Google (Analytics) acts as a third party and may process data outside the European Economic Area under its own terms. More information in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

This policy may be updated. Changes will be published on this page with the modification date.
