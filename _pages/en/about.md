Dr. Xavier Baró holds a degree in Computer Science Engineering from the [Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB)](https://uab.cat) (2003), an M.Sc. in Computer Vision from the UAB's Computer Vision Center (2005) and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the UAB (2009). He has been an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science of the [University of Barcelona](https://ub.edu) since 2024. Before that he was an Associate Professor at the [Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC)](https://uoc.edu) (2010-2024) and a part-time associate professor at the UAB (2007-2013) and the UB (2008-2016). He was a member of the UOC's [AIwell group](https://aiwell.uoc.edu). He is a member of the research group [Human Pose Recovery and Behavior Analysis (HUPBA)](https://hupba.com), of the UB's recognised research group (SGR) on Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, and of the [TECNIO group Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DSAI)](https://datascience.ub.edu/tecnio/). He collaborates on machine learning and human behaviour analysis projects (*Looking at People*), with applications to well-being, education and sport. He has organised ChaLearn competitions and datasets and has worked on biometrics for online assessment (European project TeSLA).
